 AI-Powered Sales & Business Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive **AI-driven business intelligence dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, and business operations. The project combines **Python, SQL, Power BI, DAX, and Generative AI** to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

 Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive view of business performance through interactive KPIs, data visualizations, trend analysis, and AI-generated insights. It helps users understand **what is happening in the business, why it is happening, and where improvement opportunities exist**.

The dashboard analyzes key business metrics including **Total Sales, Profit, Orders, Customers, Profit Margin, Sales Trends, Product Performance, Category Performance, Regional Performance, and Customer Segments**.

 Key Features

Sales Performance Analysis — Analyze sales trends and overall revenue performance.
Profitability Analysis-Track profit, profit margin, and identify profitable/low-profit areas.
* Customer Analysis — Understand customer segments, purchasing behavior, and contribution to revenue.
* Product Analysis — Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
* Regional Analysis — Compare sales and profitability across regions and markets.
* Interactive KPIs— Total Sales, Profit, Orders, Customers, and Profit Margin.
* Time-Series Analysis — Analyze monthly and yearly sales and profit trends.
* AI-Powered Insights — Generate business-focused explanations, observations, and recommendations from analyzed metrics.
* Interactive Filters — Filter analysis by Year, Region, Market, Category, and Segment.
* Business Recommendations — Convert analytical findings into actionable decisions.
